I'm a systems and infrastructure engineer with interests spanning data centre
networking and machine learning infrastructure. I graduated with a BSc in
Computer Science from Trinity College Dublin.

I enjoy going deep on problems at the intersection of these — how networks
behave under distributed AI workloads, why systems fail in ways that are hard
to see, and how to build tooling that makes the invisible visible.

Most of my work lives at the systems layer. I built KubeScope, a
kernel-level network observability platform using eBPF and Cilium that captures
East-West traffic flows in containerised distributed systems without sidecar
proxies. I've also been researching congestion control failure modes in AI
training fabrics — studying how ECN behaves under GPU collective incast
patterns, where the failure modes are and why they're hard to detect in
production.

Outside of that I like picking up a paper or a problem I don't fully understand
and sitting with it until I do. That's usually where the interesting work
starts.
